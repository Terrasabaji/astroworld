## 1. Education Promise

### Primary Cuspal Analysis

| Cusp | Sub-Lord Signifying | Result |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| 4th CSL | Houses 4, 9, 11 | Higher education assured |
| 4th CSL | Houses 4, 11 only (no 9) | Education limited to intermediate level |
| 4th CSL | Houses 4, 9 only (Sthira lagna) | Stops at 9th standard |
| 4th CSL | Houses 3, 8, 12 | Education denied or severely limited |
| 4th CSL | Mars/Saturn connected to 8, 12 | No schooling at all |
| 4th CSL | Houses 4, 6, 9, 11 | Higher education with exam success |
| 9th CSL | Houses 4, 9, 11 | Higher education confirmed |
| 9th CSL | Connected to 8th house | Higher education negated |
| 11th CSL | Houses 4, 9, 11 | Fulfillment of educational desire |
| 11th CSL | Retrograde | Desire unfulfilled |

### Education-Supporting Houses

- **4th house**: Basic/school education, regular attendance
- **9th house**: Higher education (PG, Ph.D.), research, thesis
- **11th house**: Success, fulfillment of ambition, passing exams
- **6th house**: Hard work, competitive exams, victory over competition
- **2nd house**: Vaksthana (speech/learning), family education tradition
- **3rd house**: Mental inclination, memory, concentration

### Education-Denying Houses

- **3rd**: Negates 4th (basic education); **5th**: End of education, negates 6th (effort)
- **8th**: Negates 9th (higher education); **12th**: Loss, withdrawal from studies

### Combined Sub-Lord Requirement

Both 4th and 9th cusp sub-lords must simultaneously signify houses 4, 9, 11 for higher education. If only one connects while the other does not, higher education will not materialize.

### Education Classification by Level

| Level | CSL Connection |
|-------|---------------|
| Post-Graduate / Ph.D. | 4th + 9th CSL → 4, 9, 11 strongly |
| Graduation | 4th CSL → 4, 9, 11 |
| Intermediate / Diploma | 4th CSL → 4, 11 only |
| Primary / High School | 4th CSL → 4 only or weak |

| House | Career Significance |

| 6th | Service, employment, daily attendance, wages, labour |

### 10th CSL Analysis

- 10th CSL in star of planet signifying 2/6/10 + 11th connection → Job certain
- 10th CSL connected to 6th → Service/employment
- 10th CSL connected to 7th → Business
- 10th CSL connected to both 6th and 7th → Both at different periods
- Sign + star + sub of 10th cusp collectively indicate profession type

### 11th CSL Analysis

- 11th CSL signifying 2, 6, 10, 11 → Employment desire fulfilled
- 11th CSL retrograde → Desire unfulfilled (exceptions apply)
- 11th cusp in movable sign → Immediate result; common sign → normal timing; fixed sign → delayed

### Material Trinity (2, 6, 10)

Houses 2 (income), 6 (daily labour), 10 (position/fame) constitute the material trinity. Employment occurs when significators of all three operate in conjoined DBA periods with 11th house support.

## 10. Timing

### Education Timing

| Event | DBA Requirement |
|-------|----------------|
| Education continues | DBA lords signify 4th or 9th |
| Education breaks | DBA lords signify only 3, 5, 8, 12 |
| Line decided | DBA at age 15-17 (intermediate) |
| Joining college | DBA → 4, 9, 11 + transit |
| Passing exam | DBA at result time → 4, 9, 11 |
| Education ends | DBA of significators of 3, 5, 8 |

### Employment Timing

| Event | DBA Requirement |
|-------|----------------|
| Getting job | DBA of significators of 2, 6, 10, 11 |
| Promotion | DBA of significators of 2, 6, 10, 11 (esp. 6+11) |
| Transfer | DBA of significators of 3, 10, 12 |
| Job change | DBA → 9, 12 in sub + 2, 6, 10 in star |
| Job loss | DBA of significators of 1, 5, 8, 9, 12 |

### Transit Confirmation Rules

1. **Sun** is the exciting planet — transits constellation of Dasanatha for time window
2. **Moon** is the fructifying planet — transits star of significator for exact date
3. On event day: Moon in star of significator; day lord matches significator
4. Dasanatha transit absolutely necessary — must transit favorable constellation
5. Planet transiting in aspect to natal position gains strength to deliver results
6. Sensitive points ruled by Dasa + Bhukti + Antara lords; event when all three transit those points

### Ruling Planets Confirmation

Ruling planets at time of judgment filter significators: Day lord, Moon's nakshatra lord, Moon's rasi lord, Lagna sign lord, Lagna star lord. Common planets between significators and ruling planets are confirmed. Reject ruling planets in sub of retrograde planets. Nodes in signs of ruling planets replace them.

### Saturn's Delaying Effect

- Gives things in installments, with delay, never to full extent
- As dasa lord causes delays; always fails first attempt
- Not aspecting a planet allows it to give results freely
- Squaring natal position: incapable of offering results
- In trine/sextile to natal position: gives results
- Aspecting 11th cusp: further delay in fulfillment

### VMD Period Allocations

Sun=6, Moon=10, Mars=7, Mercury=17, Jupiter=16, Venus=20, Saturn=19, Rahu=18, Ketu=7 years. Total 120. Shorter dasa planets (Sun, Mars, Ketu) give brief career windows; longer ones (Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Mercury) sustain extended professional periods.

## Appendix: Quick Reference Decision Trees

**Will Native Get Higher Education?**
- 4th CSL signifies 4, 9, 11 AND 9th CSL signifies 4, 9, 11 → Higher education confirmed
- 4th CSL signifies 4, 9, 11 but 9th CSL does not → Limited to graduation
- 4th CSL signifies only 4, 11 → Intermediate level only
- 4th CSL signifies 3, 8, 12 → Education denied/minimal

**Will Native Get Employment?**
- 10th CSL in star of planet signifying 2/6/10 + 11th connection → Job certain
- DBA of significators of 2, 6, 10, 11 → Timing of employment
- Neither 10th CSL nor 6th CSL connected → Employment problematic

**Service or Business?**
- 10th CSL constellation lord in 6th house → Service
- 10th CSL constellation lord in 7th house → Business
- Both 6th and 7th connected → Both at different times
- 6th house stronger than 7th → Service; 7th stronger → Business
