# Marriage KP Rules Technical Manual

Reference manual for KP (Krishnamurti Padhdhati) rules governing marriage, married life, separation, and progeny. Rules compiled from five source texts with page references.

---

## 1. Marriage Promise / Denial

### 7th Cusp Sub-Lord (CSL) Theory

The 7th cusp sub-lord is the **deciding factor** for whether marriage is promised or denied.

| Condition | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| 7th CSL signifies houses 2, 7, 11 | Marriage promised |
| 7th CSL signifies houses 1, 6, 10 | Marriage denied |
| 7th CSL connected to 6 and 12 | Marriage denied or troubled |
| 7th CSL in barren sign (Gemini, Leo, Virgo) | Weakens marriage promise |

**Source:** `kp_rules_653.json` pp.63-74; `kp_rules_655.json` pp.108-111; `kp_rules_657.json` pp.219-223

**Key rules:**
- The sub-lord of the 7th cusp must be a significator of houses 2 (family), 7 (spouse), or 11 (fulfillment) for marriage to take place. (`kp_rules_655.json` p.108)
- Houses 1 (12th to 2), 6 (12th to 7), and 10 (12th to 11) are denial houses — they negate the marriage-promising houses. (`kp_rules_655.json` p.108)
- If a planet is significator of 2, 7, 11 AND also a strong single significator of 6th house, it will NOT give marriage. (`kp_rules_655.json` p.148)
- 7th CSL in 12th cusp is weakened but cannot by itself deny marriage — additional factors (Saturn aspect, barren sign) are needed. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.158-160)

### Barren and Fruitful Signs

| Type | Signs | Effect on Marriage/Progeny |
|------|-------|---------------------------|
| Barren | Aries, Gemini, Leo, Virgo | Reduce fertility and marriage promise |
| Fruitful (Watery) | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Support marriage and child birth |
| Semi-fruitful | Taurus, Libra, Capricorn, Sagittarius | Moderate support |

**Source:** `kp_rules_653.json` pp.73, 162; `kp_rules_657.json` p.4

- Planet in barren sign weakens marriage/progeny promise even if it signifies 7th cusp.
- Planet in fruitful sign supports marriage. Ketu in watery sign linked to Saturn = marriage when Saturn agrees.

### Saturn-Ketu Denial Combination

The strongest denial formula for marriage:

> **7th cusp + Saturn-Ketu link + barren sign + 6/12 connection = No marriage**

**Source:** `kp_rules_653.json` pp.159-167

Specific conditions:
- 7th CSL Saturn is also sub-lord of 6th and 12th cusps
- Moon is in star of Saturn
- 7th lord Venus aspected by Saturn from barren sign
- Venus in star of Sun (under Saturn's control)

When all these combine, the chart "literally does not promise marriage." (`kp_rules_653.json` p.164-167)

Additional denial indicators:
- Jupiter in star of Ketu (barren sign) becomes weak to offer marriage. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.163)
- Saturn aspecting 7th lord Mercury (weak near 12th cusp) + Mercury in star of Saturn blocks marriage completely. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.163)
- Venus (Kalathrakaraka) rendered weak by combustion (within 3° of Sun) who signifies 10th bhava = denial. (`kp_rules_657.json` p.223)

### Denial Confirmation — All Three Cusps

All three cusps (2nd, 7th, 11th) and their sub-lords must be examined. If all three are rendered weak:
- Sub-lord of 2nd gets 1st house signification (negating family)
- Sub-lord of 7th gets 6th house signification (negating partnership)
- Sub-lord of 11th gets 10th house signification (negating fulfillment)

Marriage is completely denied. (`kp_rules_657.json` pp.220-223)

## 2. Node Behavior (Rahu/Ketu)

### Priority Order for Rahu/Ketu Results

Rahu and Ketu give results in strict priority order:

1. Planet **conjoined** with the node
2. Planet **aspecting** the node
3. **Star lord** (constellation lord) of the node
4. **Sign lord** of the node

**Source:** `kp_rules_653.json` pp.14-15

### Node Agency Rules

| Rule | Details | Source |
|------|---------|--------|
| Nodes are stronger than planets they represent | Rahu representing Venus replaces Venus and gives results more strongly | `kp_rules_655.json` p.178; `kp_rules_657.json` p.270 |
| Nodes have no aspect in KP | Unlike Mars (4,8), Jupiter (5,9), Saturn (3,10), nodes do not aspect | `kp_rules_653.json` p.12 |
| If node not conjoined or aspected, it offers sign lord results | Rahu in Libra alone → gives Venus results | `kp_rules_655.json` p.110 |
| Rahu/Ketu are Chaya Grahas (shadowy planets) | They reflect results of planets they connect with | `kp_rules_655.json` p.110 |

### Planets in Node Stars

Planets deposited in node constellations give results as if they were the node:
- **Rahu stars:** Arudra, Swati, Satabhisha
- **Ketu stars:** Ashwini, Makha, Moola

Any planet transiting these stars will offer results of Rahu/Ketu respectively. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.16)

### Reading 7th CSL When It Is a Node

If 7th cusp sub-lord is Rahu or Ketu:
1. Check planet conjoined with it → read that planet's results
2. If no conjunction, check planet aspecting it → read that planet's results
3. If neither, take the lord of the star in which the node is deposited

**Source:** `kp_rules_655.json` p.99

### Node Gender Classification

Rahu, Ketu, Mercury, and Saturn are classified as **eunuch** planets. When eunuch planets connect to 7th cusp with male/female planets in specific configurations, unconventional relationships are indicated. (`kp_rules_653.json` pp.140-148)

## 3. Love Marriage

### 5th-7th-11th Connection

For love marriage, the 5th cusp (romance) must connect to the 7th cusp (marriage):

- **5th CSL must be linked to 7th cusp** — if 5th CSL signifies 7th or 11th, love marriage is successful. (`kp_rules_657.json` p.243)
- **7th CSL must connect back to 5th cusp** — mutual connection required. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.45)
- **Venus must signify 11th cusp** (fulfillment of desire). (`kp_rules_653.json` p.45)

**Discrimination between love and arranged marriage:**
- Love marriage: 7th CSL connected with 5th and 11th houses
- Arranged marriage: 7th CSL connected with 2nd and 11th houses

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` p.35

### Venus-Mars-Saturn Combinations

| Planet | Role in Love/Romance |
|--------|---------------------|
| Venus | Rules romantic life, chief governor of marriage |
| Mars | Rules passions and physical desire |
| Saturn | Rules secrecy in love affairs |

Venus as sub-lord of 7th cusp AND owner/significator of 5th cusp, with Saturn involvement = secret love affair leading to marriage. (`kp_rules_657.json` pp.20-21)

### Love Marriage Denial

- If 5th CSL signifies 6th house → lover withdraws from the affair
- If 5th CSL signifies 12th house → querent drops the idea of love marriage

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.244-245

### Timing Love Marriage

For love marriage timing, consider significators of houses **2, 5, 7, and 11** (the 5th is added to standard marriage houses). (`kp_rules_657.json` p.35-36)

## 4. Second Marriage

### 9th Cusp Analysis

The 9th cusp (being 3rd from 7th) represents the second partner / next relationship. Check 9th CSL for second marriage prospects. (`kp_rules_653.json` pp.168-170)

### Dual Sign / Mercury Rule

Two conditions must be simultaneously satisfied for second marriage:

1. **7th CSL in dual sign** (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces), OR in constellation of planet in/owning dual sign, OR 7th CSL is Mercury
2. **7th CSL is also significator of 2nd or 11th house**

If either condition fails, second marriage is denied. (`kp_rules_657.json` pp.94, 251-253; `kp_rules_655.json` pp.183-185)

### 2-9-11 Signification

- 2nd cusp sub-lord connected with 7th house → second marriage
- 2nd cusp sub-lord connected with 11th house → keeping a partner (not legal marriage)
- 7th CSL in dual sign as significator of Marakasthana and Badhakasthana → wife passes away earlier; same rule applies for 2nd wife via 2nd CSL

**Source:** `kp_rules_655.json` pp.148, 86

### Marrying a Divorcee/Already-Married Person

Saturn (unconventional) + Mars (courage) + Mercury (duality) connected to 7th house + 8th house (extra nature) → indicates marrying a divorcee. (`kp_rules_653.json` pp.134-136)

## 5. Separation / Divorce

### 6-12 CSL Connection Rule

> If 6th cusp sub-lord and 7th cusp sub-lord are linked → marriage is troubled / divorce likely.
> If they are NOT linked → marriage sustains.

**Source:** `kp_rules_653.json` pp.68, 215-216, 221-223

### Separative Planet Identification

| Planet | Separation Role |
|--------|----------------|
| Saturn | Forces separation, delay, cold behavior |
| Ketu | Moksha node — denotes break, exit, abort |
| Mars | Quarrels, aggression, courage to separate |

The combination: **Saturn + Ketu + Mars connected to cusps 6, 7, 12** = divorce. (`kp_rules_653.json` pp.222-223)

### Houses for Separation/Divorce

- Houses **1, 6, 10, 12** are detrimental for marriage
- 6th house → separation from partner's side (partner walks out)
- 12th house → native seeks separation (goes away)
- 8th house → quarrels and difficulties but NOT actual separation

**Source:** `kp_rules_655.json` pp.156-157, 193-196

### Divorce Timing

Marriage ends in separation when DBAS planets signify **both** marriage houses (2, 7, 11) AND detrimental houses (6, 12). (`kp_rules_653.json` p.64)

Divorce formula: VMD planets signifying 6, 7, 12 with involvement of Rahu/Ketu and Saturn trigger divorce. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.223)

### Marriage Sustainability Test

For marriage to sustain after it occurs:
1. 6th CSL and 7th CSL should NOT be linked to each other
2. Succeeding dashas should not signify 6/12 linked to 7th
3. If succeeding dashas signify 11th cusp → marital life continues

**Source:** `kp_rules_653.json` pp.215-217

## 6. Timing

### Ruling Planets Theory

Ruling planets at time of judgment:
1. Lagna sign lord
2. Lagna star lord
3. Moon sign lord
4. Moon star lord
5. Day lord

These ruling planets guide selection of fruitful significators for timing. Common planets between significators of 2, 7, 11 and ruling planets are selected as the operative significators. (`kp_rules_653.json` p.14; `kp_rules_657.json` pp.79-82, 205-207)

### Significator Hierarchy (4-Step Process)

Priority order for finding significators of any house:

| Priority | Level | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| 1 (Strongest) | PCO | Planets in constellation of **occupants** of the house |
| 2 | OCC | **Occupants** of the house |
| 3 | PCBL | Planets in constellation of **lords** of the house |
| 4 (Weakest) | BL | **Lords** (owners) of the house |
| 5 | — | Planets conjoined with or aspecting any of the above |

**Source:** `kp_rules_655.json` pp.112-113, 120-121; `kp_rules_653.json` all examples

### Transit Methodology — KP Transit Rule

> A planet transiting in a constellation brings to light matters shown by the lord of that constellation. The **sub** shows whether the result is favourable or not.

**Source:** `kp_rules_656.json` pp.146-147, 171, 219

Key principles:
- The **transiting planet** = source/channel
- The **constellation lord** = nature of result (WHAT happens)
- The **sub lord** = deciding factor (WHETHER it happens favorably)

### Jupiter/Saturn Sub-Transit for Marriage

At time of marriage, **all planets** must transit in constellation and sub of significators of houses 2, 7, 11. (`kp_rules_656.json` pp.363-364)

**Jupiter transit through marriage significator star — stages by sub:**

| Sub Lord | Stage |
|----------|-------|
| Mercury sub | Horoscopes received, advertisement |
| Venus sub | Boys come to see girl |
| Sun sub | Gold ornaments purchased |
| Moon sub | Negotiation concluded |
| Mars sub | Marriage fixed |
| Rahu sub | Wedding purchases/arrangements |
| Jupiter sub | **Marriage celebrated** |
| Saturn sub | Boy takes leave, guests depart |

**Source:** `kp_rules_656.json` pp.210-211

**Saturn transit sub for marriage timing:**
- Saturn in Moon's sub → marriage negotiated and concluded
- Saturn in Venus's sub → marriage celebrations occur
- Saturn in Jupiter's sub → marriage of children celebrated

**Source:** `kp_rules_656.json` pp.332, 345-346

### Dasha-Bhukti-Antara Selection

Marriage takes place during conjoined periods of significators of houses 2, 7, and 11:
- **Dasa lord** shows general trend
- **Bhukti lord** narrows the period
- **Antara lord** pinpoints further
- **Sookshma lord** pinpoints the day

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.22-23, 91; `kp_rules_655.json` pp.113-114

Selection methods:
1. **Ruling Planets method** — compare significators with ruling planets at judgment time (preferred for future events)
2. **Sub-based selection** — verify sub-lord of each significator connects to 2, 7, 11 (useful for past event verification)

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` p.91; `kp_rules_655.json` p.120

### Pinpointing the Exact Day

- **Sun's transit** through constellation/sub of significators → indicates the **month**
- **Moon's transit** through constellation/sub of significators → indicates the **day**
- **Ascendant** rising in sign/star/sub of significators → indicates the **moment**

During A Dasa, B Bhukti, C Antara: sensitive points are A-sign B-star C-sub (or permutations). Sun, Moon, and Lagna must transit these points. (`kp_rules_656.json` pp.195-196, 216, 220, 225-226)

### Traditional Transit Rules Rejected

KP explicitly rejects:
- Gurubalam (Jupiter must be in 2,5,7,9,11 from Moon)
- Chandrashtama (Moon in 8th from natal Moon)
- Sade-Sati (7.5 years Saturn)
- Vedha system
- Sign-based transit results

Only the **constellation and sub** of the transiting planet matter. (`kp_rules_656.json` pp.360-364)

## 7. Retrograde Effects

### Sub-Lord Retrograde Behavior

The golden rule from KP 6th Reader (p.192):

Three conditions for progeny promise — if ANY fails, child birth is denied:
1. Sub-lord of 5th cusp is **not retrograde**
2. Sub-lord is **not in constellation of a retrograde planet**
3. Sub-lord is a significator of 2nd, 5th, or 11th house

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.24-25, 217

### Practical Experience vs Theory

Despite the golden rule, multiple verified cases show retrograde planets giving results:
- Retrograde sub-lord does not necessarily constitute an absolute bar to fructification
- Jupiter (ruler) in sub of retrograde Saturn at time of event still gave desired result
- More research needed on retrogression's actual effect

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.210-211, 217

### Significator Retrograde and Delays

- Saturn (whether retrograde or not) causes **delay** but not denial when it is a significator of marriage houses. (`kp_rules_655.json` p.111)
- Saturn's Punarphoo effect: whenever Saturn connects with Moon (by aspect, conjunction, or constellation), delay is caused in sub-periods. (`kp_rules_657.json` p.93)
- Retrograde transiting planet forming same aspect multiple times → gives same type of result repeatedly (negotiation → fixation → celebration). (`kp_rules_656.json` p.23)

### General Treatment in KP

Throughout the source texts, retrograde planets are evaluated using the same star-sub-sign lord chain as direct planets. Retrograde status is noted but the signification chain determines results. (`kp_rules_653.json` — observed across all example charts)

## 8. Progeny

### Houses 2/5/11 for Child Birth

| House | Significance |
|-------|-------------|
| 2nd | Kutumbhasthana — family growth, addition of members |
| 5th | Progeny, conception, creative power |
| 11th | 5th from 7th (partner's fertility), permanent ties, fulfillment |

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.4-5; `kp_rules_655.json` pp.213, 232

### The Golden Rule for Progeny

Three conditions must be **simultaneously** satisfied:

1. Sub-lord of 5th cusp is NOT retrograde
2. Sub-lord is NOT in constellation of a retrograde planet
3. Sub-lord is a significator of 2nd, 5th, or 11th house

If ANY one condition fails → no child birth at all.

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.24-25 (from KP 6th Reader, Horary Astrology, p.192)

### Additional Denial Conditions

- 5th CSL in barren sign (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Virgo) → weakens progeny
- 5th CSL in star of Rahu or Ketu (abortive nodes) → negates progeny
- 5th CSL connected to 4th house (12th to 5th) → denial
- 5th CSL connected to 8th or 12th houses → denial

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.202-203; `kp_rules_655.json` pp.232-233

### Fertility Planets

| Gender | Monthly/Fertility | Vitality |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| Female | Mars (periods), Moon (fertility) | — |
| Male | — Sun (vitality) | Venus (fertility) |

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.30-31, 261

Jupiter (Putrakaraka/Brihaspati) is the chief governor for child birth. Jupiter must be examined for strength — if in star of abortive Rahu/Ketu or in barren sign, it negates progeny despite being natural karaka. (`kp_rules_657.json` pp.9-15)

### Male vs Female Horoscope Assessment

- In **male** chart: examine 11th CSL for progeny (being 5th from 7th = wife's fertility)
- In **female** chart: examine 5th CSL directly

If male's 11th CSL promises child birth but 5th CSL does not → husband may have children elsewhere, not with legally married wife. (`kp_rules_657.json` pp.234-235)

### Twin Birth Conditions

All three conditions must be simultaneously satisfied (rare event):

1. 5th CSL in constellation of planet in dual sign (Gemini, Pisces, or first half of Sagittarius)
2. Constellation lord in star of planet in dual sign
3. Sign of 5th cusp must be dual or in star of planet in/owning dual sign

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` p.148

### Sex Determination of Child

**Method 1 — Hora:**
- 5th CSL or its star lord in Sun Hora → male child
- 5th CSL or its star lord in Moon Hora → female child

In odd signs: first 15° = Sun Hora, next 15° = Moon Hora. In even signs: reversed.

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.228-231

**Method 2 — Star/Sub of constellation lord:**
- Constellation lord in star/sub of male planets (Sun, Mars, Jupiter) → male child
- Constellation lord in star/sub of female planets (Venus, Moon, Rahu) → female child

**Source:** `kp_rules_657.json` pp.147-148

### Timing of Child Birth

Child birth occurs in joint period of significators of houses 2, 5, and 11. Moon's transit in star/sub of significators pinpoints the date of delivery. Moon governs the uterus, ovaries, impregnation, and birth timing. (`kp_rules_657.json` pp.87, 136-137, 165, 226-227)

### Denial of Children — Traditional Malefics

If Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, Mars, or Mercury are in 5th house without favorable connections:
- Saturn → controlling/delaying
- Rahu/Ketu → abortive
- Mars → aggressive/destructive
- Mercury → effeminate

If lords of 2, 5, or 11 are conjoined with or aspected by these planets without positive mitigation, children are denied. (`kp_rules_655.json` p.214)
