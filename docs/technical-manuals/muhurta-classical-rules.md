## 1. Tithi Classification

### Tithi Groups

| Group | Tithis | Lord | Meaning | Suitable Activities |
|-------|--------|------|---------|-------------------|
| Nanda | 1, 6, 11 | Venus | Happiness | Celebrations, festivities, love |
| Bhadra | 2, 7, 12 | Mercury | Fortunate | Wealth, business, trade |
| Jaya | 3, 8, 13 | Mars | Triumph | Competitions, exams, disputes |
| Rikta | 4, 9, 14 | Saturn | Empty | Surgery, debt payment, destruction |
| Purna | 5, 10, 15 | Jupiter | Full | Education, harvesting, abundance |

### Paksha Preference

- **Shukla Paksha** (waxing) — Productive acts, growth, prosperity
- **Krishna Paksha** (waning) — Completion, releasing, letting go
- Krishna Paksha 1–5: as favorable as Shukla equivalents; 6–10: middling; 11–15: avoid
- Exception: 1st Tithi only favorable in Krishna Paksha (Moon combust in Shukla 1st)

## 2. Unfavorable Tithis

**Generally Unfavorable**: 4th, 6th, 8th, 9th, 12th, 14th, 15th (6th only slightly). Exception: Jupiter/Venus in/aspecting Lagna counteracts.

**Parva Tithis** (4th, 8th, 12th, 15th): Sun-Moon at 0°/90°/180°/270° — transitional, avoid for beneficial acts.

**Dark Tithis**: Shukla 1st (Moon combust); Krishna 11th–15th (Moon very waned).

**Kshaya Tithi**: Starts after sunrise and ends before next sunrise (missing sunrise) — unfavorable.
**Adhi Tithi**: Same tithi at two consecutive sunrises — unfavorable.

**Last 3 Tithis** of a lunar month: avoid. Exception: Jupiter in friendly Rasi in angle.

**Adhika Masa**: Intercalary month unfavorable for lasting events. Exception: Sun exalted/friendly + Jupiter in own shadvarga.

## 3. Vishanadi Periods

Each Tithi has a 4-Nadi poison period (Tyajya) at specific Sun-Moon degree separations.

**Effects**: 1st Nadi = ruin; 2nd = life harm; 3rd = ruins everything; 4th = family prosperity harm.

### Vara Vishanadi (Start Nadi after sunrise)

| Sun | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 30 | 42 | 41 | 31 | 2 | 22 | 20 |

Calculation: Divide Vara length by 60 for one Nadi duration, then apply offset.

## 5. Vara (Weekday)

### Vara Nature

| Vara | Planet | Nature | Type |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Sunday | Sun | Fixed | Dhruva |
| Monday | Moon | Moveable | Chara |
| Tuesday | Mars | Fierce | Ugra |
| Wednesday | Mercury | Mixed | Misra |
| Thursday | Jupiter | Swift | Kshipra |
| Friday | Venus | Gentle | Mridu |
| Saturday | Saturn | Sharp | Tikshna |

Each Vara favorable for activities matching its nature and same-class Nakshatras.

**Unfavorable**: Sun/Mars/Saturn Varas for beneficial events. Sun only slightly unfavorable. Mars not unfavorable after midday. Exception: Vara lord strong and well-disposed.

**Three Tithis/Nakshatras in one Vara**: Events lack consistency — may prove disastrous.

### Gulika Timing

Divide daytime into 8 parts. Assign planets starting from Vara lord in weekday order. Saturn's portion = Gulika (unfavorable). Exception: Jupiter in angle nullifies.

### Hora (Planetary Hour)

24 Horas per Vara; first ruled by Vara lord, then: Saturn→Jupiter→Mars→Sun→Venus→Mercury→Moon (repeating). Effects: Sun=danger, Moon=well-being, Mars=quarrels, Mercury=fruition, Jupiter=gains, Venus=enjoyment, Saturn=setbacks.

## 6. Nakshatra Classification

### By Nature

| Type | Nakshatras | Activities |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Mridu (Soft) | Mrigasira, Chitra, Anuradha, Revati | Gentle/joyful events, ceremonies, ornaments, singing |
| Dhruva (Fixed) | Rohini, Uttaraphalguni, Uttarashadha, Uttarabhadrapada | Lasting things — foundations, planting, coronations, peace |
| Chara (Moveable) | Punarvasu, Svati, Sravana, Dhanishtha, Satabhisha | Change, ephemeral things, riding, gardens |
| Kshipra (Swift) | Asvini, Pushya, Hasta | Trade, sports, education, arts, medicine, journeys |
| Tikshna (Sharp) | Ardra, Aslesha, Jyeshtha, Mula | Surgery, attacks, imprisonment, separation |
| Ugra (Fierce) | Bharani, Magha, Purvaphalguni, Purvashadha, Purvabhadrapada | Destruction, enemies, weapons, arson |
| Misra (Mixed) | Krittika, Visakha | Combined activities |

Cross-compatibility: Dhruva↔Mridu, Chara↔Kshipra, Ugra↔Tikshna.

### Facing Directions

| Facing | Nakshatras | Suitable For |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Urdhva (Up) | Rohini, Ardra, Pushya, U.Phalguni, U.Ashadha, Sravana, Dhanishtha, Satabhisha, U.Bhadrapada | High-rise, coronations, flags |
| Adho (Down) | Bharani, Krittika, Aslesha, Magha, P.Phalguni, Visakha, Mula, P.Ashadha, P.Bhadrapada | Digging, wells, mines |
| Tiryag (Horizontal) | Asvini, Mrigasira, Punarvasu, Hasta, Chitra, Svati, Anuradha, Jyeshtha, Revati | Travel, plowing, vehicles |

### Special Rules

- **Pushya**: Most favorable for ALL beneficial activities except marriage. Neutralizes all blemishes.
- **Generally Unfavorable**: Bharani, Krittika, Ardra, Aslesha, Magha, Purvaphalguni, Jyeshtha, Purvashadha, Purvabhadrapada.
- **Malefic Proximity**: Nakshatra with malefic + one before/after = unfavorable. Exception: Jupiter aspecting malefic.
- **Last 2 Nadis** (26m40s) of any Nakshatra: unfavorable — events lack stability.

### Ravi Yoga

Moon in 4th, 6th, 10th, 13th, or 20th Nakshatra from Sun's Nakshatra — destroys all unfavorable influences.

| Vara | Nakshatras |

### Tripushkara and Dvipushkara

- **Tripushkara**: Malefic Vara + Bhadra Tithi (2,7,12) + Tripada Nakshatra (Krittika, Punarvasu, Uttaraphalguni, Visakha, Uttarashadha, Purvabhadrapada) → event repeats 3×
- **Dvipushkara**: Malefic Vara + Bhadra Tithi (2,7,12) + Dvipada Nakshatra (Mrigasira, Chitra, Dhanishtha) → event repeats 2×

Favorable for repeatable events; avoid for one-time events (marriage).

### Combination Strength

- Vara/Tithi/Nakshatra: destroys 1000 adversities (9× Vara/Tithi)
- Vara/Nakshatra: destroys 300 adversities (3× Vara/Tithi)
- Vara/Tithi: destroys 100 adversities (3× Tithi/Nakshatra)

### Abhijit Muhurta

8th of 30 Tithyamsas (middle of first Karana) — grants success despite other unfavorable factors. Usable on any Vara except Wednesday.

## 9. Lagna Selection

### Favorable Lagnas

Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius, Pisces (benefic Rasis). Cancer avoid when Moon within 60° of Sun.

### Unfavorable Lagnas

Aries, Leo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Aquarius (malefic Rasis). Exception: Jupiter/Venus in/aspecting Lagna overcomes.

### Sirodaya vs. Pristodaya

| Type | Rasis | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| Sirodaya (Head-first) | Gemini, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Aquarius | Fulfills readily and sooner |
| Pristodaya (Feet-first) | Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn | Slower manifestation |
| Ubhayodaya (Both) | Pisces | Neutral |

### Malefic Positions to Avoid

- No malefics in Lagna or Navamsa Lagna
- Mars NOT in 8th house (Mahadosha #6) — destroys before maturity
- Venus NOT in 6th house (Mahadosha #5) — unrequited desires
- Malefics in angles = Sarpa Yoga (difficulties)
- Benefics in angles = Mala Yoga (smooth unfoldment)
- Malefics best in 3rd, 6th, 11th; 8th/12th best empty
- Exception: Venus/Moon in 10th + Jupiter in angle overcomes afflicted Lagna

### Blind, Deaf, and Lame Rasis

| Condition | Day | Night |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Blind | Aries, Taurus, Leo | Gemini, Cancer, Virgo |
| Deaf | Libra, Scorpio | Capricorn, Sagittarius |
| Lame | Aquarius | Pisces |

Exception: Strong Mercury/Jupiter/Venus in 4th, 7th, or 10th removes.

### Lagna–Natal Relationship

Lagna must not be 8th from natal Lagna/Moon of individual or spouse (Mahadosha #11). Exception: Lords of natal sign and 8th being same/friendly.

### Navamsa Lagna

- Must be of a benefic planet
- Must not be Suunya Rasi
- Lord in angle or 11th (avoid 6th/8th/12th)
- Even more important than Rasi Lagna for success level

## 10. Panchanga Shuddhi

Requires favorable Tithi, Karana, Vara, Nakshatra, and Yoga with well-disposed lords (Mahadosha #1).

### Panchanga Lord Functions

| Component | Lord Governs |
|-----------|-------------|
| Tithi | Enjoyment, happiness |
| Karana | Capacity to get things done |
| Vara | Creative force, vitality |
| Nakshatra | Healthy growth |
| Yoga | Holds event together |

Lords must be: exalted/own sign/friendly sign, aspected by benefics, not in dusthanas, not debilitated.

**Remedial**: Sun/Moon in 11th sanctifies all Panchanga components.

### Major Doshas

- **Gandaanta** (Mahadosha #7): Last 48 min of Revati/Aslesha/Jyeshtha + first 48 min of Asvini/Magha/Mula; last/first 24 min of Purna/Nanda Tithis; last 3° of Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces + first 3° of Aries/Leo/Sagittarius
- **Sankranti** (Mahadosha #3): 3 Saura days before/after Cancer/Capricorn ingress; 16 Nadis before/after other ingresses
- **Eclipse**: Avoid 3 days before/after. Eclipse Nakshatra avoided 6 months (full)/3 months (half)/1 month (quarter). 7 Nakshatras before/after form Graha Suula.
- **Vyatipata/Vaidhriti**: Must absolutely avoid. Occur once/month each.

### Jupiter/Venus Combustion

Avoid marriage, education, construction, deity installation, coronations when Jupiter/Venus combust, retrograde, debilitated, or in inimical Rasis. Both combust = beyond remedy. Avoid 3–10 days before/after combustion (old/childhood stages).

### Panchaka Dosha

(Tithi# + Nakshatra# + Vara# + Lagna#) / 9. Remainder: 1=death, 2=fire, 4=king, 6=thief, 8=disease. Remainders 3,5,7,9 not unfavorable.

### Three Paryayas

- **1st** (Nakshatras 1–9): Full effects
- **2nd** (10–18): Half negative; only avoid 1st Pada of Vipat, 4th Pada of Pratyak, 3rd Pada of Vadha
- **3rd** (19–27): Negligible negative effects

### Chandrabala

Moon must NOT be in 6th (delays), 8th (destruction — Chandra Ashtama), or 12th (disrupts) from natal Moon. Exception: Lords of natal Moon sign and 8th being friends.

### 88th Nakshatra Pada

Must always be avoided. Exception: Lagna and 10th lords being friends nullifies.

## 12. Activity-Specific Rules

### Marriage

- **Nakshatra**: Mridu/Dhruva. Uttaraphalguni specifically favorable. AVOID Pushya.
- **Tithi**: Shukla Paksha. Avoid Tripushkara/Dvipushkara.
- **Karana**: Kaulava favorable
- **Planets**: Jupiter/Venus must not be combust

### Construction and House Entry

- **Nakshatra**: Dhruva (Rohini, U.Phalguni, U.Ashadha, U.Bhadrapada). Urdhva Mukha for high-rise.
- **Karana**: Gara, Taitila
- **Planets**: Jupiter/Venus must not be combust

### Education

- **Nakshatra**: Kshipra (Asvini, Pushya, Hasta)
- **Vara**: Thursday (Jupiter); Wednesday (Mercury) for sciences/arts
- **Tithi**: Purna (5, 10, 15). Shukla Paksha.
- **Planets**: Jupiter/Venus must not be combust

### Travel

- **Nakshatra**: Chara/Kshipra. Tiryag Mukha favorable.
- **Vara**: Monday (moveable). Avoid Saturday.
- **Exceptions**: Avoid Asvini-Tuesday, Pushya-Thursday, Rohini-Saturday

### Medical

- **Surgery**: Tikshna Nakshatras (Ardra, Aslesha, Jyeshtha, Mula). Rikta Tithis. Mars Vara acceptable.
- **Treatment**: Kshipra Nakshatras. Avoid Roga Panchaka.

### Business and Trade

- **Tithi**: Bhadra (2, 7, 12)
- **Karana**: Vanija
- **Nakshatra**: Kshipra
- **Vara**: Wednesday (trade), Thursday (large ventures), Friday (luxury goods)

## Neutralization and Remedial Factors

### Primary Neutralizers

1. Jupiter/Venus well-dignified in Lagna — subdues all harmful forces
2. Benefic (not Moon) in Lagna not debilitated — overcomes most ills
3. Sun in 11th (day) or Moon in 11th (night) — powerful remedial force
4. Lagna or Moon in Vargottama — removes all Doshas
5. Beneficial Vara/Tithi/Nakshatra Yogas — single most powerful granters of success

### Vargottama Definition

1st Navamsa of moveable signs, 5th of fixed signs, 9th of dual signs (Rasi and Navamsa identical).

### Planet Well-Disposed

Exalted, Mulatrikona, own Rasi/Navamsa, Vargottama, aspected by exalted/own-sign planet, aspected by benefics.

### Planet Poorly Disposed

Debilitated, with malefics, debilitation Navamsa, Fatal Degree (Mrityubhaga), in 6/8/12 from own placement, last degree of Rasi.

### Fatal Degrees (Mrityubhaga)

**Sun**: Ari=20°, Tau=9°, Gem=12°, Can=6°, Leo=8°, Vir=24°, Lib=16°, Sco=17°, Sag=22°, Cap=2°, Aqu=3°, Pis=23°

**Moon**: Ari=26°, Tau=12°, Gem=13°, Can=25°, Leo=24°, Vir=11°, Lib=26°, Sco=14°, Sag=13°, Cap=25°, Aqu=5°, Pis=12°

### Sakata Yoga

Moon in 6th/8th from Jupiter = delays/disappointments. Exceptions: Jupiter/Venus strong in Lagna; Moon in own/exaltation/dual Rasi; Jupiter in angle; Moon 5th + Jupiter 10th = Raja Yoga.

### Gochara (Favorable Transit Houses from Natal Moon)

Sun:3,6,10,11 | Moon:1,3,6,7,10,11 | Mars:3,6,11 | Mercury:2,4,6,8,10,11 | Jupiter:2,5,7,9,11 | Venus:1-5,8,9,11,12 | Saturn:3,6,11

Vedha positions can block favorable effects. Father/son pairs (Sun-Saturn, Moon-Mercury) don't cause Vedha to each other.

### Janma Masa

Birth lunar month should be avoided for life-long events. Exception: Natal Lagna lord and month lord in friendly Rasi, aspected by benefics.
